### BMA Digital Platform — Condensed Executive Summary (Mizo Tawng)

#### 1. Thiltum (Purpose)

**BMA Digital Platform** hi **web + mobile** platform khaikhawm vek a ni a, Bangalore-a Mizo khawtlang tana chanchin thar, membership, leh inbiakpawhna hmunpui (official hub) ni tura duan a ni.

**Goal:** Khawtlang in-thruihawhna tihchak, Mizo tawng leh hnam identity vawnhrin, leh BMA hnathawh dante security rintlak tak hnuaia tih-modernize (bilingual digital platform hmangin).

#### 2. Platform-in a pek chhuah turte (What the Platform Delivers)

##### Member-te Tan:

- Bilingual experience (**Saptawng + Mizo tawng**).
- Chanchin thar (News), events, announcements, leh thlalak dante (photo galleries).
- BMA chungchanga zawhna leh puihna dilna lo chhang thei tu **24/7 AI help desk**.
- Hmun tina hman theih (web, Android, iOS).

##### BMA Leadership Tan:

- Hmun pakhata **content & membership management** enkawlna.
- Online membership payments (UPI, cards, netbanking).
- WhatsApp & email notification-te.
- Analytics dashboard (membership & engagement thlirpui na).
- Role-based access control hmanga hman dan chanchin (audit trail) felfai tak vawnna.

#### 3. Launch Strategy (Risk Tlemna)

- **March 21, 2026:** Full **web launch rintlak** tak neih a ni ang.
- Mobile apps tlangzarh leh chu **thla 2–3 hnuah** (App Store / Play Store) a ni ang.
- Member-ten a ni hmasa ber atangin mobile browser hmangin an hmang nghal thei dawn a, App Store phalna nghah chhung pawhin kan launch a khaihlak dawn lo a ni.

#### 4. Budget & Costs (Kum 1-na)

##### **Budget Recommend zat: ₹1,45,000**

Hemi chhungah hian:

- Platform setup & accounts.
- Siam chhuahna (Development - thla 2).
- Launch hnua support pek chhunzawmna.
- Kum 1-na operating cost leh thil thut thut atana reserve (buffer).

**Launch hnua kum khat operating cost:** ₹36,000 – ₹86,000 (tlangpuiin).

#### 5. Sum leh Pai (Financial Sustainability)

##### Membership Revenue (A tlem ber tura chhutin)

- Member 1,500 × ₹350 = **₹5,25,000**
- Fees leh platform cost zawng zawng hnuah → **₹4.3L+ surplus** (BMA-ah a lut thei ang).

##### Revenue Belh theihna dangte

- Tualchhuing sumdawnnate sponsorship.
- In-app advertisement (duh thlan theih).

**A rah chhuah:** Platform hian **ama sum a in-enkawl zo** ang a, BMA tan sum lak luhna thra tak a ni ang.

#### 6. Engvangin nge thutlukna thra a nih?

- **Data ownership:** BMA-in member-te chanchin (data) a thunun vek dawn.
- **Hnam identity vawnhrinna:** Mizo tawng full support.
- **Hnathawh rangna:** Kut-hnathawh (manual work) a tlem dawn.
- **Thalaite hipna:** Modern tak leh hman awlsam tak.
- **Professional image:** BMA rintlakna a tipuar dawn a ni.

#### 7. Risks & Mitigation (Hlauhthawnna leh a hmanruate)

- **Technical:** Cloud platform secure leh rintlak tak hman a ni ang.
- **Financial:** Budget fimin buffer neih tel a ni.
- **Operational:** Admin tools hman awlsam leh AI help desk.
- **Vendor lock-in:** Open standards hman a nih dawn avangin nakinah a hmun sawn (migration) a harsa lo ang.

#### 8. Thutlukna Mamawh (Decision Requested)
