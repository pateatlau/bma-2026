### BMA Digital Platform — Condensed Executive Summary

#### 1. Purpose

The **BMA Digital Platform** is a unified **web + mobile** platform designed to become the **single official hub** for communication, membership, and engagement for the Mizo community in Bangalore.

**Goal:** Strengthen community engagement, preserve Mizo language and culture, and modernize BMA operations through a secure, bilingual digital platform.

#### 2. What the Platform Delivers

##### For Members

- Bilingual experience (**English + Mizo**)
- News, events, announcements, and photo galleries
- **24/7 AI help desk** for BMA-related questions and request for BMA assistance
- Works on any device (web, Android, iOS)

##### For BMA Leadership

- Centralized **content & membership management**
- Online membership payments (UPI, cards, netbanking)
- WhatsApp & email notifications
- Analytics dashboard (membership & engagement)
- Full audit trail with role-based access control

#### 3. Launch Strategy (Low Risk)

- **March 21, 2026:** Full **web launch guaranteed**
- Mobile apps released **2–3 months later** (App Store / Play Store)
- Members can access via mobile browsers from Day 1
- No dependency on app store approvals for launch success

#### 4. Budget & Costs (Year 1)

##### **Recommended Budget: ₹1,45,000**

Includes:

- Platform setup & accounts
- Development (2 months)
- Post-launch support
- Year 1 operations with contingency buffer

**Annual operating cost after launch:** ₹36,000 – ₹86,000 (typical)

#### 5. Financial Sustainability

##### Membership Revenue (Conservative)

- 1,500 members × ₹350 = **₹5,25,000**
- After fees & platform costs → **₹4.3L+ surplus**

##### Additional Revenue

- Local business sponsorships
- Optional in-app advertisements

**Result:** Platform is **self-funding** and generates surplus for BMA.

#### 6. Why This Is a Good Decision

- **Data ownership:** BMA controls all member data
- **Cultural preservation:** Full Mizo language support
- **Operational efficiency:** Reduced manual work
- **Youth engagement:** Modern, accessible platform
- **Professional image:** Enhances BMA credibility

#### 7. Risks & Mitigation

- **Technical:** Proven, secure cloud platforms
- **Financial:** Conservative budgeting with buffer
- **Operational:** Simple admin tools + AI help desk
- **Vendor lock-in:** Open standards enable future migration

#### 8. Decision Requested

Approval for:

1. **Budget:** ₹1,45,000 (Year 1)
2. **Launch date:** March 21, 2026 (Web)
3. **Team formation:** Development, Content, Legal/Compliance
